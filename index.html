Research

I work on world models — neural networks that learn to simulate an environment well enough for an agent to plan and learn inside them. My recent focus is an open-source PyTorch reimplementation of DreamerV4: a masked-autoencoder video tokenizer, a block-causal transformer dynamics model with flow-matching prediction, and imagination-based policy optimization (PMPO), trained end-to-end on cloud GPUs.

That work included diagnosing a temporal-collapse failure in the tokenizer's latent cross-attention and resolving it with frame-based rotary position embeddings. Current projects: mechanistic interpretability of world-model latents — what control-oriented dynamics models actually encode about physical state — and a diagnostic study of when offline imagination-RL outperforms behavior cloning.
